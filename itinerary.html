Prechecks & Red Alerts

San Francisco

Check bridge-wind conditions, parking availability, and waterfront closures before a Golden Gate walk. Fog and cold gusts can make the city feel much colder than the temperature.

Big Sur / 17-Mile Drive

Start with a slow SFO arrival and a 1.5-day city rhythm, then head south for the 17-Mile Drive and Big Sur coastline. This is the gentlest scenic leg before moving inland toward whichever national park weather makes sense for.

Sequoia / Redwood decision

Choose Sequoia National Park if snow and road conditions are manageable. Choose Redwood National Park if weather turns rough, the mountain routes are unsafe, or the Sierra is closed. This keeps the trip flexible without forcing a risky pass.

Weather safety

Do not push into mountain sections when roads are snowed in, chains are required, or the park is closed. If the Sierra route looks rough, pivot to the coast and redwoods instead of forcing a risky detour.

Winter law

California winter driving rules can require chains or snow tires in Sierra-adjacent routes. Check conditions before each mountain segment and never force a pass when it is closed or icy.

Weather + essentials

  • Weather: Spend about 1.5 days in SFO, then move south for the 17-Mile Drive and Big Sur. Use the weather to choose Sequoia if the Sierra is clear, or Redwood National Park if conditions turn rough.
  • Essentials: Winter jacket, fleece layers, hat, gloves, insulated shoes, reusable water bottle, power bank, snacks, charger, tire chains, emergency blanket, and a snow-ready AWD or 4WD setup if you decide on Sequoia.
  • Red alerts: If the mountain roads are snowed in or closed, skip Sequoia and pivot to Redwood instead. Do not force a pass just to keep a fixed plan.
