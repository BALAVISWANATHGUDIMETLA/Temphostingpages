Prechecks & Red Alerts

San Francisco

Check bridge-wind conditions, parking availability, and waterfront closures before a Golden Gate walk. Fog and cold gusts can make the city feel much colder than the temperature.

Big Sur / Carmel

Fuel up before leaving the city, verify CA-1 closure alerts, and avoid cliffside pull-offs in wet conditions. Coastal roads can look dramatic and still be risky in winter fog or rain.

Sequoia / Kings Canyon

Check park alerts, daylight travel windows, and snow conditions. Cold nights, icy patches, and elevation changes make early starts and layered gear essential.

Mammoth (optional)

Only go if snow conditions, road status, and chain requirements are favorable. If weather is unstable, skip the detour and keep the trip on a safer route.

Winter law

California winter driving rules can require chains or snow tires in Sierra-adjacent routes. Check conditions before each mountain segment and never force a pass when it is closed or icy.

Weather + essentials

  • Weather: Start in SF with cool urban weather, then move to the warmer coast, then head into Sequoia where the nights get much colder and drier.
  • Essentials: Winter jacket, fleece layers, hat, gloves, insulated shoes, reusable water bottle, power bank, snacks, charger, tire chains, and emergency blanket.
  • Red alerts: Winter mountain driving is weather-dependent, so keep daylight-only travel, check chain requirements, and skip any optional high-country detour if roads are icy or closed.
